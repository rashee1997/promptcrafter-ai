# Image & Logo Prompt Studio

**Key Features**
- Dual‑mode form: Image (AI‑generated art) and Logo (brand‑identity) pipelines.
- Curated style recipes & logo archetypes with one‑click presets across categories (Editorial, Cinematic, 3D, Fine Art, Sci‑Fi, Architecture; Tech & SaaS, Modern, Luxury, Creative, Artisan) plus an AI‑assisted template generator.
- Image‑to‑prompt reverse engineering – upload an image and receive an engineered descriptive prompt; edit‑prompt flow revises an existing image prompt.
- Logo Studio 2.0 brand workbench – Smart Brand Autopilot strategist, brand guidelines card, logo mockup deck, and favicon simulator.
- Tiered settings layout: Essentials always visible → Refine accordion (platform dialects & brand sub‑card) → Art Direction (lighting, camera, palette, etc.).
- AI‑suggested negative prompts – one‑click generation of a brief‑specific exclusion list, appended with commas.
- Copy all prompts – copies all platform dialects as one clipboard payload with inline "Copied ✓" feedback.
- Save to gallery – persists generated briefs locally for later comparison.
- Custom chip values and save‑for‑later – every setting (style, lighting, mood, composition, camera, color grade, resolution, aspect ratio, platform dialects, logo‑specific rows) supports a "Custom…" entry.

**Usage Guide**
1. **Choose mode** – toggle between Image and Logo.
2. **Pick a style** – select from the Essentials grid or open Refine for platform dialects and brand sub‑settings.
3. **Describe your subject** – free‑form text or AI‑refreshed example chips matched to the current module.
4. **Art Direction** – customise lighting, camera, composition, mood, colour‑grade; negative prompt line with one‑click AI suggestion.
5. **Generate** – prompt streams with platform‑specific dialect cards (DALL‑E 3, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Flux, Nano Banana) and a copy‑all action.
6. **Save to gallery** – persist the brief locally; revisit or copy later.
