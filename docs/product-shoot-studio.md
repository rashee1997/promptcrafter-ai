# Product Shoot Studio

**Key Features**
- Isolated sub‑tab of the Video studio for cinematic product‑video ad prompts.
- Upload product reference images and fill a short brief (name, category, description, key selling point, audience).
- Scene recipes – curated one‑click concepts plus a "Surprise me" option.
- Creative art‑direction controls – camera motion, lighting, surface materials, physics FX, motion pace, human interaction, focal length, aspect ratio, target duration.
- Multi‑dialect output deck – formatted prompts for Runway, Kling, Veo, Luma, and Minimax.
- Extension beats – chain past single‑clip limits for 10 s + videos.
- Audio foley, ad strategy, and 3‑shot campaign arcs – design packages, strategy notes, and hook → feature → CTA storyboard.
- Local persistence – saved shoots with favorites, automatic quota‑exceeded fallback that drops thumbnails but keeps prompt text.

**Usage Guide**
1. **Open the sub‑tab** – navigate to Video → **Product Shoot**.
2. **Upload images & fill brief** – add product images (client‑side compression) and enter name, category, description, key selling point, audience.
3. **Pick a scene recipe** – choose a curated concept or let the AI suggest one.
4. **Adjust art‑direction** – tweak camera motion, lighting, materials, FX, pace, aspect ratio, and target duration.
5. **Generate** – receive a multi‑dialect prompt package (Runway, Kling, Veo, Luma, Minimax) plus optional clip‑extension beats.
6. **Remix or save** – iterate on concepts with the remix loop or persist the shoot to the local gallery.
